# Stakeholder Management & Productization Strategy Review

Leadership review for scaling an enterprise AI assistant from pilot to adoption across the organization.

## 👉 [Read the Strategy Memo](./STRATEGY_MEMO.md)

## What's inside

| Section | Contents |
|---|---|
| 1. Stakeholder Map | Influence/interest matrix with benefits, concerns, resistance and strategy for each stakeholder |
| 2. Engagement Plan | Communication approach, owner, cadence, escalation path and success indicators |
| 3. Change & Adoption Scorecard | 10 dimensions, from readiness to realized business value, with baselines, targets and go/no-go gates |
| 4. Productization Strategy | Target users, value proposition, MVP, service boundaries, onboarding, support, governance, funding, roadmap and risks |
| 5. 90-Day Action Plan | 12 prioritized actions across three 30-day phases, with owners and exit criteria |

## Frameworks used
PMI stakeholder analysis · Prosci change metrics · Kotter's 8-step change model · Product lifecycle (Harvard DCE, Atlassian)

> Note: pilot baseline figures are illustrative planning assumptions.
